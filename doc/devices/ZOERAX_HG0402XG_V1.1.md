### Installation

Using SPI clamp on-board is the only method for initial installation.

### What works

- All four 2.5GBASE-T RJ45 ports at 10/100/1000/2500 Mbps
- SFP ports with various 1G/10G Fiber and Ethernet modules 
- LEDs
